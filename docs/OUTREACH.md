# Validation Outreach — landing one real quote / LOI

Goal (per PLAN.md §1a move #2): one on-the-record sentence from a real debris-monitoring ops manager or a county finance/emergency-management contact, confirming that load-call disputes cost real money. One quote on a slide converts the Impact axis from "plausible" to "validated." A signed letter of intent is the stretch goal.

**Run this in parallel with the build (PLAN.md §8a, days 1–9). It is scheduled work, not optional.**

---

## What counts as a win (in priority order)

1. A **letter of intent / design-partner agreement** — strongest. Even one sentence: "We'd pilot video-verified load tickets on our next event."
2. A **quotable line** we can show on a slide, attributed by role (not name, unless they OK it): *"A single deobligation cost us six figures and we couldn't defend the paper tickets." — County finance director, Gulf Coast.*
3. A **data point**: disputes per event, dollar value of their last deobligation, hours spent on monitoring labor.

Even an anonymized quote is fine. Ask: "Can I quote that, attributed to your role?"

## Email template A — monitoring firm (challenger to the incumbents)

> **Subject:** Quick question on load-ticket disputes
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> I'm building LoadTruth, a tool that turns tower-camera video of each dump truck into a verified FEMA load ticket — automatic fill-% measurement, debris classification, and an evidence frame behind every number. The idea is Loadscan-grade verification from a phone or pole camera, so a firm can win fixed-fee bids with fewer monitors in the tower.
>
> I'm not selling anything. I'm trying to make sure I'm solving the right problem before I go further, and you've run the real thing.
>
> Could I ask you two quick questions?
> 1. Roughly how many load-call disputes do you see per event?
> 2. When a call gets challenged on audit, what does that cost you — in hours, or in dollars?
>
> Even a one-line reply helps. Happy to show you a 2-minute demo of where it's at if it's useful.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your name] · [LinkedIn / GitHub]

## The 2 questions that matter most

Keep every conversation anchored on these — they produce the slide:
1. **Disputes per event** (frequency → the problem is real and recurring).
2. **Dollar value of the last deobligation / dispute** (magnitude → the Impact number).

## Follow-up that converts a reply into a quote

> This is really helpful — thank you. Two things:
> 1. Would you be open to a 15-minute look at the demo next week? I'd value your read on whether the evidence trail matches what an auditor actually needs.
> 2. Can I quote your point about [their specific pain] on a project slide, attributed just to your role and region (e.g. "County finance director, Gulf Coast") — no name unless you prefer?
